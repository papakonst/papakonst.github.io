Projects

6D Pose Estimation for Robotic Arm Grasping — Irida Labs & SUPSI (2023–2025)

Delivered a ROS-based computer vision pipeline for high-accuracy 6D pose estimation using RGB-D (ToF). The system employs a two-stage approach—object detection followed by a foundation pose estimator—to enable robust grasping across varying lighting and backgrounds. Integrated camera calibration and validation loops to ensure repeatable accuracy in real deployments.

Smart Cooking Device — Data & AI Solution (2023–2025)

Co-developed an embedded AI stack for a next-generation kitchen appliance: food classification, placement guidance, and robust inference on constrained hardware. Augmented scarce data with diffusion-based generation and few-shot techniques to improve generalisation across ingredients, cookware, and lighting conditions.

RGB Stereo Scanner with Pattern Projector — Irida Labs (2022–2023)

Designed a high-precision stereo 3D scanner (2× RGB + pattern projector) in collaboration with Gizelis Robotics and LMS (HybridR). Achieved sub-millimetre accuracy and implemented hand-eye calibration to register the sensor in the robot base frame for reliable manipulation and inspection.

Industrial Quality Inspection at the Edge — Irida Labs (2021–2022)

Built a production-grade CV system on NVIDIA Jetson, integrating three cameras and a PLC. The pipeline captures products via photocells, detects defects in real time, and signals the PLC to stop the line on failures. Emphasis on low latency, robust operation, and clear diagnostics for operators.

Multi-Camera Pose Estimation for Warehouse Monitoring — Irida Labs (2020–2021)

Developed a multi-view 6D pose estimation system by combining object detection and keypoint estimation, followed by solvePnP. Registered poses from many cameras into a common 3D reference frame to track assets and events at scale.

Instrument-Playing Humanoid Robot (M.Sc. Thesis) — TU Delft (2017–2018)

Implemented computer-vision-based detection and 6D localization of a metallophone for the NAO humanoid robot, coupled with visual servoing to execute precise striking motions. Included camera calibration and kinematic modelling (forward/inverse) for end-to-end accuracy.

Line-Following Mobile Robots — Robotics Club, University of Patras (2015–2016)

Team project to build competitive line-following robots. Led algorithm design, software development and testing; contributed to sensor selection, integration, and optimisation; and tuned PID control for speed and stability on challenging tracks.
